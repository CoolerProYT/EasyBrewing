  - title: Brew from a stack
    details: Potions stack to 16. Drop a stack in the station and it keeps brewing three at a time until the stack runs out.
  - title: Speed upgrades
    details: Three tiers that stack together. Five Speed Upgrade 3s brew in just over a second.
  - title: Amount upgrades
    details: Brew more potions from every ingredient and every blaze powder.
  - title: Built for automation
    details: Choose which slot each side of the block feeds. Hoppers and pipes work on every side.
  - title: Every brewing recipe
    details: Uses the game's own brewing recipes, so recipes added by other mods work too, and on 26.3 datapacks as well.
  - title: Works with other mods
    details: JEI and REI recipe pages, Create's mechanical arm, and Cobblemon's brewing recipes, depending on your version.
